Our Story

Made slowly,
to live with for years.

MDM Furniture began in Addis Ababa in 1998 with a simple belief: furniture should be made for decades, not seasons.

We work with a small circle of artisans across Ethiopia and East Africa. Timber is chosen board by board. Leather is cut by hand. Nothing leaves the atelier until it feels inevitable — quiet, tactile, and entirely at home.

Our materials are traceable and our finishes are non-toxic. We build in small runs, repair what we make, and design each piece to age gracefully — because the most sustainable furniture is the furniture you never want to replace.
